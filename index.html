What is measured

Calendar days from the date a leader formally left their top office to the date they formally held that office, or its equivalent, again. Handover dates are those of the successor taking office and of the leader being sworn in, reinstated or named CEO again.

Limitations

Dates are rounded to the day and can differ by a day between time zones. Steve Jobs left as Apple chairman and returned as interim CEO. Entries are a curated selection, not an exhaustive list.
