# Be Right Back

Every leader leaves. A few are back before the coffee gets cold. Or maybe the coffee has already gone bad?

A ranking of how quickly leaders of well-known companies, organisations and governments regained power after losing it. 80 entries, six languages (English UK/US, Gaeilge, 日本語, 繁體中文 and Kashur, an invented cuneiform language).

- `index.html`: description, featuring now, top 15
- `ranking/index.html`: full ranking with filters
- `assets/data.js`: the data; `assets/i18n.js`: strings and Kashur; `assets/app.js`, `assets/style.css`
